# Governance

AssembleJS is maintained by the ayers-software-repair organization on GitHub. There is one
maintainer role today. Decisions on scope, design and releases are made by the maintainers and
recorded in docs/adr/. Contributions follow CONTRIBUTING.md; the Code of Conduct applies to every
space the project runs.

Becoming a maintainer: sustained, reviewed contributions over time, then an invitation from the
existing maintainers. There is no other path.
